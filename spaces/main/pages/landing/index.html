What is di.iiii?

di.iiii is where you make a 3D space and hand out its address. Build scenes, place objects, set up lighting and cameras, and invite others into the same space in their browser.

Create

Add 3D shapes, import models and images, write text in 3D space. Arrange everything with drag-and-drop controls.

Collaborate

Invite anyone with a link. See live cursors and changes. Work together across the world without any setup.

Publish

Every space has a public URL. Share the link — visitors see your work in their browser or in a VR/AR headset.

How to use di.iiii

You can be building your first 3D scene in under two minutes.

1

Open a space

Click “Step inside” — you get a space of your own, no account needed. Sign in to keep it and to make more.

2

Add objects

A space holds projects — open one, then use the Create window for 3D shapes, text, images, or 3D models. Drag to position them.

3

Set up the scene

The Scene window sets the sky, background and lighting. Select an object and edit it in the Objects window.

4

Share or publish

Copy the space link to invite collaborators, or publish to make it live for the public.

Made for everyone

di.iiii works for artists, developers, organisers, and automated systems alike.

Artists & creators

Build 3D exhibitions and installations a visitor opens from a link. No 3D software experience needed.

Developers

Extend with node-based logic, connect via the serverXR API, embed in your own site, or build agent-driven experiences.

Event organisers

Create virtual venues, stage previews, and spatial layouts for live events, conferences, or art shows.

AI agents

Access scene state, modify objects, and read space data via the serverXR REST API. Machine-readable endpoints at /serverXR/api/.

What you can do

Objects and nodes on one canvas

Every object is a typed node. Wire them, group them, script them.

Real-time collaboration

See teammates’ cursors and changes live, in the same space.

WebXR ready

Enter VR or AR from any supported browser — no app install.

Asset pipeline

Upload images, 3D models, audio. Optimised and served automatically.

Spaces

A space is a place that is yours — its own address, its own guest list, and the projects you make in it. Share by link. Lock editing or leave it open.

Publish anywhere

Each space has a public URL. Export JSON. Embed or link directly.

Guest & sandbox modes

Visitors explore without an account — a shared global space, or a private sandbox each.

3 free spaces

Sign in and create up to three of your own spaces for free. Admins are unlimited.
