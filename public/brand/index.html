using the mark

  • Square corners, no radius. The mark is a square holding a smaller square — an aperture. Every surface repeats it.
  • Cyan is the only accent. Never a second hue.
  • Clearspace: keep one mark-height of empty space on all four sides.
  • Never stretch, skew, rotate or recolour off-palette; no shadow, glow or bevel.
  • Type: Inter for everything, JetBrains Mono for labels and captions in uppercase.
