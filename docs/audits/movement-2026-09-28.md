# Movement & look audit — BEFORE (dev @ 6d415945), 2026-09-28

What the first-person walker feels like to someone walking a venue: the start
and stop, the weight of the camera, bob, a slow look across a hall, flying up
to the trusses, and the phone joystick. The numbers come from the repeatable
rig in [`scripts/movement-rig/`](../../scripts/movement-rig/README.md). That
rig runs a headed Chromium on the RTX 3080 inside a private nested KWin
display, at DPR 1.5, in two rooms:

- a synthetic calibration room;
- the **MOXIR hall**, from bundle sha256 `ee2a5976d70a18a5…`, which is pinned.

The evidence frames are in [`movement-2026-09-28/`](movement-2026-09-28/).
Each file is a 12-frame contact sheet (4×3, read left→right, top→bottom).

## Rerun against any branch

```bash
cd ~/work/di.iiii-movetest            # branch test/movement-rig
node scripts/movement-rig/run.mjs --ref feat/elite-move \
    --moxir-bundle .movement-rig/moxir-2026-09-28.space-bundle.tar.gz
node scripts/movement-rig/report.mjs .movement-rig/before-dev/results.json .movement-rig/<run>/results.json
```

`--ref` measures a clean detached checkout of that commit, and `--worktree <dir>`
measures a working tree. The bundle file is local and gitignored. Without
`--moxir-bundle`, the rig re-exports MOXIR from `~/.local/share/di.iiii/data`,
and the hash in `results.json` shows whether the hall changed. Every browser
session waits for the CPU package to be ≤ 85 °C, holds the machine-wide
browser lock, and refuses SwiftShader or llvmpipe.

## What was measured, and how far to trust it

- **Movement physics, look gain and linearity** (speeds, ramps, stops, deg/count)
  come from the camera matrix of every frame the renderer drew. They do not
  depend on machine load. Two independent runs agree within 5 % (below).
- **Frame pacing is not an idle machine's.** Run 1 had load average ≈ 22 on 16
  threads, the CPU package at 98–100 °C, and other agents' browsers running;
  the owner was gaming. The synthetic room reached 60.0 fps with p99 17.7 ms
  in an earlier, cooler run (11:58 UTC) and 35 fps in the busy one. MOXIR
  frame times need a rerun on a cool machine; the rig records load and
  temperature per session, so that rerun will say whether it was clean.
- **The look gain is in `movementX` units** (browser counts). The step from a
  physical mouse count to `movementX` is not measured here: `xdotool` gets 0
  movement through Xwayland+libei in the nested display. See the README
  limits and the calibration that is still owed.
- XR is listed, not measured (no headset here).

## Ranked defects, by eye and by number

1. **Nothing is solid.** Walking forward goes straight through the Gate box.
   The camera log runs z 26 → 14.5 against a face at 20.1, and sheet frame 5
   is solid yellow: [synthetic-walk-start-stop.jpg](movement-2026-09-28/synthetic-walk-start-stop.jpg).
   On the phone, walking up the MOXIR nave ends *inside* the DJ booth. Frames
   9–12 are the inside of the booth box:
   [moxir-phone-joystick-walk.jpg](movement-2026-09-28/moxir-phone-joystick-walk.jpg).
   In a venue you walk into walls, the barrier and the stage.
2. **Flying down goes under the floor.** Altitude is clamped at −2 m, not at
   the floor. The camera ends at −1.14 m (synthetic) and −0.81 m (MOXIR), and
   the last 4 frames of both fly sheets are the void under the floor:
   [synthetic-fly-truss-and-back.jpg](movement-2026-09-28/synthetic-fly-truss-and-back.jpg),
   [moxir-fly-truss-and-back.jpg](movement-2026-09-28/moxir-fly-truss-and-back.jpg).
3. **A slow, careful look loses pointer lock.** Sweep at 2 counts per frame,
   the way you take in a hall. After 30 such moves, the broken-lock detector
   (`BROKEN_LOCK_DEAD_DELTA_MAX = 4`, `BROKEN_LOCK_DEAD_MOVES = 30`) decides
   the lock is dead and exits it. Only 25 % of the turn happens, and the view
   drops to drag-look. The same thing makes "200 counts as 100 × 2" deliver
   0.30 / 0.11 of the angle. The Wayland workaround fires on a healthy lock,
   whenever the hand moves slowly.
4. **Look is ~30× too fast for this kind of walking.** 0.67° per count is
   1.7 cm/360 at 800 DPI, CS2 sens 30.5. A 1 cm hand movement turns the view
   about 210°. The transfer is otherwise clean: linear, no accel, no
   smoothing, 1 frame to settle.
5. **Head bob is 10 cm peak-to-peak at 1.5 Hz.** It is visible as the horizon
   bouncing during a walk. Game walks sit at a few cm or none; for looking at
   a venue, bob reads as seasickness.
6. **The first ~0.3 s after the click that locks is dead.** 312–334 ms of look
   input is thrown away (`BROKEN_LOCK_SETTLE_MS` is counted from the first
   locked move), so the first flick loses 20–60°.
7. **The walk is a run, and it slides to a stop.** Top speed is 5.1 m/s. The
   ramp is linear (t50/t90 ≈ 0.5–0.6): no ease-in, a hard corner at top
   speed. The stop is a linear 10 m/s² slide of 1.2–1.3 m, which the eye reads
   as ice. Diagonal is √2 faster (1.31–1.38×).
8. **Vertical fly has no weight.** Full speed in 16–41 ms and a dead stop in
   43–58 ms: the camera jerks up and down. Leaving fly in the air floats back
   to eye height over 1.4 s on an exponential lerp, where a fall is expected.
9. **The wheel teleports.** One notch is a 1.0 m jump in one frame, which the
   eye reads as a judder step, not a dolly.
10. **Phone.**
    - The joystick's x axis *turns* (89 °/s) and cannot strafe (0 m sideways).
    - Joystick t90 is 460–530 ms, against 280–350 ms on the keyboard.
    - A swipe across the screen turns 112°.

    The sheet shows the joystick and hints reading clearly:
    [moxir-phone-joystick-walk.jpg](movement-2026-09-28/moxir-phone-joystick-walk.jpg).
11. **MOXIR frame pacing (†, unconfirmed).** 23.6 fps with p99 92 ms while
    walking the hall, on a hot and loaded machine. The slow look sweep sheet
    shows no tearing or skipped geometry, but its frame spacing is 8 s for 191
    frames: [moxir-look-sweep-slow.jpg](movement-2026-09-28/moxir-look-sweep-slow.jpg).
    Rerun cool before acting on it.

Seen and **fine**:

- no drift after release;
- heading follows the view instantly with no side-slip (heading vs travel 0°);
- strafing past the MOXIR columns shows no tearing
  ([moxir-strafe-past-columns.jpg](movement-2026-09-28/moxir-strafe-past-columns.jpg));
- the arrow-key turn stops dead on release (0° overshoot).
