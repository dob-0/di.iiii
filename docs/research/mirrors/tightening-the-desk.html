Tightening the Desk

The ranked plan from the 2026-08-21 deep audit of Raw — ten agents: six researching TouchDesigner, cables.gl, Blender/Unreal, touch-first tools, minimal-density design and the 2025–26 field; four walking Raw itself at phone and desktop size, counting every gesture. Every finding below carries screenshot or code evidence, and the load-bearing ones were re-verified by hand.

4.3%of a blank desktop screen does any work. On the phone, one node card is 51% of the screen's width.
12 → 8steps for the commonest full task (place, wire, see a result) — a third of the work is recoverable without adding one control.
0gestures exist today for renaming a node. The verb is missing entirely — the schema supports it, the UI never offers it.

Track 1 — the defect wave · verified bugs, fix first

Small, safe, high-value. All confirmed on screenshots I read myself.

Phone stackingevery double-tap placement clamps into a ~108px band, so new nodes land on top of each other and off-screen right. Scale the clamp to real screen width.bug
Fake fullscreenentering the Scene with a node selected keeps the inspector sheet over 38% of the room — with an armed Delete button floating over the stage. Clear selection on entry.bug
No way outthe fullscreen exit is an unstyled 21px default-HTML button — on a phone in zen mode it is effectively invisible. Style it like its 44px siblings.bug
Tap-empty ≠ deselectphones have no Escape key, and tapping empty canvas never closes the inspector. One call to the existing clearSelection from the background-tap handler.bug
Example lands unfit"All nodes example" drops 94 nodes and leaves the view where it was — spaghetti with most of the graph off-screen. One line: call the existing fit after insert.bug
Palette off-screensummoned near an edge, the palette runs past the viewport with no scroll cue. Clamp its position like any menu.bug
Windows hide their cardButton/Text/Timeline windows open at a hardcoded spot — usually on top of the very card just placed. Spawn beside the card instead.bug
Cards escapea card dragged left can carry its door button fully off-screen with no way back. Clamp drags the way placement already clamps.bug
Silent mis-dropa wire aimed at Size lands on Roughness when types don't match — silently. Say what happened, on the surface, when a drop is redirected.bug

Track 2 — short ways · the time-savers

Every tool studied converges on the same few moves. None needs new chrome — each reuses the palette, the wire drag, or the inspector that already exist.

Wire into spaceThe flagship. Dragging a wire into empty canvas today shows a dead-drop toast. Make it open the palette, pre-filtered by the wire's type; picking places the node and wires it. Standard in cables.gl, Unreal, Blender, Node-RED — and the biggest single cut to daily patching.M
Renameclick the inspector title, type, Enter — the same inline edit the number fields already use. A theatre graph fills with six Numbers and four Cubes; names are how a show stays legible.S
Desk wakes upzen hides the topbar on an empty canvas and never re-checks — so the Scene button stays invisible all session. Re-resolve when the first node appears; entering the scene drops from 4 steps to 1.S
Insert on wirelong-press or click a wire to splice a node into an existing connection — the "I need a Lag between these two" move, one gesture instead of four.M
Chain modea toggle in the palette: consecutive picks auto-wire from the previous node's output. Linear chains are most of patching; TouchDesigner users live on this.S
Duplicate, findableCmd+D exists but nothing on screen says so — add Duplicate to the palette's command list where Chat and Help already live.S

Track 3 — space that works · density without clutter

The stylesheet has no levers: 5,165 lines, every spacing a hand-typed pixel value, and ~700 lines style things that no longer exist. Build the levers first, then tighten.

Delete the dead~14% of the stylesheet paints nothing (a superseded onboarding flow, an unused dialog). Remove before any density work.S
A spacing scalesix tokens for the values already most common, plus one density variable the tightest surfaces multiply by. Turns "make it 15% tighter" from a 300-edit slog into one number.M
One geometrycard width and header height live as JS constants and separate CSS literals; wires are drawn from the JS numbers. Export once, read everywhere — otherwise cards can't ever change size safely.S
Topbar carries statethe 1440px bar holds three small clusters and ~1000px of black. Let the centre carry what's already computed: zoom, node count, scope path. No new widgets — relocation.S
Honest reservesevery window pays a flat 120px bottom margin for chrome that often isn't there; the phone placement clamp pre-reserves 45% of the height for a sheet that doesn't exist yet. Derive from what's actually mounted.M

Track 4 — the phone as a main instrument

Wiring two visible nodes one-finger already works — the 44px port targets earn their keep. What breaks is everything around it.

Tap-tap connecttap an output to arm it, tap an input to close the wire — the drag stays, this is the forgiving second path every touch node editor converges on. An armed port + palette also gives one-finger place-and-wire.M
Palette rows 44pxrows are 33px on touch — below the app's own standard, in its most-used surface. One media-query bump.S
Toolbar respects the cardthe floating zoom/delete cluster dodges the bottom sheet but lands on the selected card's own ports. Add the card's rect to the avoidance maths.S
Reachable undoundo exists only as Ctrl+Z — nonexistent on glass. One-finger reachable, likely in the same zoom cluster.S
Up & home, pinneda fixed "up a level" + "fit to view" pair in the thumb zone — the one-finger answer to deep rooms and lost graphs, straight from TD's breadcrumb keys.S

Track 5 — richness inside minimalism

From the OP-1, Linear, and Arc studies: richness that appears under intent and vanishes at rest.

Long-press peekhold a node or wire: a quiet overlay shows its live value, error state, cost. Release, gone. TouchDesigner's middle-click, made one-finger.M
Status lineone ambient line of truth (what's live, what's sending, what the clock says) in chrome that already exists — the board we built outside the product, moved inside it.M
Live value on the nodea small living number or waveform on the card itself — the single most informative pixel-per-pixel upgrade a patcher can have, and the costliest here. Worth a prototype before a promise.L

Track 6 — bigger bets · your call

App modea second face for a finished graph: only its Buttons, Timelines and Monitors, arranged full-bleed — the operator's desk without the wiring. This is where "use Raw to show the show" fully lands, and it's the field's clear 2026 direction.yours
Collapse to nodefold a subgraph into one card with its promoted ports — touches the port-promotion seam, so it wants design care, not a quick wave.yours
Spoken subgraphsdescribe a patch, get a proposal you accept or edit — the desk already has agents; the research says keep it a proposal, never an auto-apply.yours

What we will not copy

Modal parameter dialogs that block the canvas · hotkey-only power layers with no visible equivalent (locks out touch) · four parameter modes per field · essential info behind mouse-only inputs · zoom gestures that fight pinch. The field's mistakes are as instructive as its wins.

The method & the spend

This ran as the standing research method: questions first, then 10 sonnet agents in one workflow (~1.05M subagent tokens, ~24 min), structured returns only, synthesis done by the main session, everything banked to a dated ledger (RAW_UX_LEDGER.md + raw JSON) so no question here is ever paid for twice. Deep dives are earned by rank, not curiosity — nothing above needed one yet.

Proposed order: Track 1 (defect wave, immediately) → Track 2's flagship + rename + desk-wakes-up → Track 3's levers → Track 4 → Track 5 prototypes → your word on Track 6. Every wave keeps the cadence: tests, lint, build, seen-before-shipped, staging as guest.
