## 1 · The show in 5 lines

1. **MOXIR = մոխիր = ash**, what is left after a fire (owner, 2026-10-08: *"the name is moxir մոխիր, it's in Armenian — keep it in mind, and for the vibe"*). The hall is a burnt-out factory; the light rises out of it.
2. **One DJ on one low step**, in the middle of the nave, with the whole 100 m hall behind him. The crowd stands in front, toward the entry.
3. **Three colours only:** ash white, ember red, black. No rainbow, nothing moving fast overhead (owner 09-28: *"the underground rave thing, not the commercial shit"*).
4. **Elite and minimal** (owner 2026-10-08, N330/N331): touring-grade design, but every unit must serve a signature moment or it is not hung; at most 2 layers lit most of the night.
5. **Six lasers from the depth of the hall**, all beams ending on steel behind the DJ; a sloped truss ("the cut") hung from the overhead crane behind him; darkness as the main colour.

## 5 · Safety

### 5.1 Lasers (Class 4 — the maker itself says entertainment lasers should be Class 1/2/3R)
- **Permission:** held, per the owner (2026-10-08). Owed for the production file: who issued it, a copy, and the **named laser operator / safety officer (LSO)** at a kill switch with sight of every beam — not the owner, not the DJ.
- **Rules in the design:** every beam ends on steel; ≥ 3.0 m above any floor people can reach (HS(G)95) and the design holds ≥ 5.0 m; 2.5 m laterally from accessible structure; no beam past z 21; static beams only; software zones (LaserOS "Safety Zone") are the second barrier, never the first.
- **Reviewer's blockers (Fable, 10-08, sent to the v1.0 agent):** (B2) the crane girder as a stop has ~8 cm of aiming slack at 61 m and is glossy riveted steel → a dedicated **matte-black steel beam-stop panel** behind the DJ, beams aimed 0.3° above its centre, tilt screws locked, the cube's mechanical Beam Block set; (B3) 9 of 12 beams cross the unmodelled pendant-lamp layer and 2 end on walkable runways → measure the lamps today, **lock every ladder to the runways and crane walkways**; (B4) the cubes are rated **10–40 °C** (manual p. 36) and the hall may be near 0–5 °C → mains adapters, warm cases, a small heated enclosure per cube, a thermometer in the LSO log; (B5) the six interlock ports daisy-chained into **one hard-wired E-stop** at the LSO position (the non-US unit ships with a jumper = no E-stop by default), tested before every set. Status of each: **v1.0: in progress.**
- **Standards named:** IEC 60825-1:2014, IEC TR 60825-3, HSE HS(G)95, ANSI Z136.10, ILDA guidelines, the ULTRA MK2 Guide v1.0.

### 5.2 Rigging
- The cut on the crane: 3 picks ≤ 146 kg each, bridles ≤ 120°, safety steels on separate points, picks shackled to the chords (a sloped truss slides in basket slings), tie-offs with ≥ 0.5 m clearance to fixed things and 1.0 m to anything that moves (the cab). **Owed:** the crane's rating plate (SWL), inspection record, the venue's written consent, lock-out of the crane and trolley before anyone rigs (ISO 12480-1; the post-Soviet crane rules PB 10-382-00 ask a partial inspection every 12 months, a full one every 3 years — Armenian text not found, verify with the venue), a rigger's sign-off (DGUV V17/18, EN 17206, ANSI E1.2/E1.4-1).
- Ground support: the goal post for lasers 1–3 (20 kg on a 12 m span rated 290–450 kg), the 6.5 m tower for cube 6 and the plan-B post: **towers, base plates, outriggers, ballast and a stability check are owed** (ANSI E1.21, the tower maker's table).
- The cut hangs 0.2 m higher in the PONYO copy than git derives (8.0 m clamps on a 7.95 m girder) — check before the show. At a measured 7.70 m the low end drops to 2.99 m, under the 0.5 m hands margin.

### 5.3 Crowd and effects
- Barrier at z 25.8 with a 1.3 m pit; **capacity, exit widths, emergency lights, extinguishers, stewards and first aid are not in any file** — the promoter's safety lead owes them (Green Guide / Purple Guide method: 2 persons/m², 82 persons/min per metre of exit).
- Floor fixtures within 2 m of the crowd on a riser or in a pen with a steward; cable ramps; movers' pan/tilt limited in the desk; nothing on the floor between the PA and the barrier.
- Strobes: ≤ 4 flashes/s sustained; a strobe cue sheet; blinders ≤ 40 %, ≤ 2 s.
- Stewards at the runway ladders and in the far half (laser zone, machinery, pressure vessels, scrap). What is in the white bags, and can they go.
- Haze vs smoke detectors; the nearest dwelling for noise (industrial zone; WHO night guideline 45 dB as a yardstick).
