# UP-B380F, 16 channels: the TESTED map against the equivalent chart (2026-10-09)

Asked by the MOXIR lead for the v2 patch (18 × UP-B380F on U1, 16 ch each; owner N460.2): check the TESTED
16-channel map in `src/rigbuild/fixtureTypes.js` against the 16-channel chart of the equivalent head
(`scripts/place/fixtures/fixtures.json`, source `PROST380`), list every disagreement, say what is walked on a real
unit on setup day. The TESTED map wins; this note changes no map and no address.

**Result in one line.** All three charts are 16 channels wide, so no address in the patch depends on any of this
(U1.001, 017 … 273 stay). What differs is what the console SENDS on channels 2–3, 7–8 and 11–16. Ten disagreements
against the equivalent chart, four against the console's own profile; none can be settled from paper. One of them,
channel 13, can leave all 18 beams dark, so it is walked first.

Where the agreements are: channels 1, 4, 5, 9 and the shutter/dimmer values (0–3 dark, 255 open, 0–255 dimmer) are the
same wherever they sit, and A and C agree on 1–5 and 7–10 (6 is the label above). Focus sits at channel 11 (C), 14 (B) or 15 (A).

### The ten disagreements, A against B (the equivalent chart)

1. **ch 2:** A tilt · B pan fine.
2. **ch 3:** A pan fine · B tilt.
3. **ch 7:** A strobe/shutter · B dimmer.
4. **ch 8:** A dimmer · B strobe.
5. **ch 6, frost:** A reads it as linear from 0 (ASSUMED, the chart states no curve) · B inserts it only from 128.
6. **ch 10, gobo:** A 5-wide slots from 5, shake from 171 · B 4-wide slots from 4, rotate/stop 72–159, shake from 160. The same value picks a different gobo: 30 is gobo 6 on A, gobo 7 on B; 70 is gobo 14 on A, gobo 17 on B.
7. **ch 11, prism:** A prism 1 in from 128 · B prism 1 at 64–127, prism 2 at 128–191, both at 192–255.
8. **ch 13:** A prism 2 insert · B prism 2 rotation.
9. **ch 14:** A prism 2 rotation · B focus.
10. **ch 15:** A focus · B rainbow.

### The four disagreements, A against C (the console profile that ran)

1. **ch 6:** A frost · C `gobo2`. The names in C are the console's own labels, so this may be a label only; it is walked.
2. **ch 11:** A prism · C focus.
3. **ch 12:** A prism 1 rotation · C prism.
4. **ch 13:** A prism 2 insert from 128 · C "must be 145 or the head emits no light". Under A, 145 reads "prism 2 in".

Channels 14–16 are `aux` in C: it is silent there, so there is nothing to contradict, and nothing to confirm.

**An old hint that the chart does not support.** `fixtures.json` suggests ch 13 "must be 145" may be a lamp strike. On B the
lamp commands are on **channel 16** (100–105 off, 200–205 on, 240–255 reset) and 145 on ch 13 is prism-2 rotation (128–187,
forward). So the equivalent chart does not explain the 145; it stays unexplained until the unit is stepped.

## What the TESTED map wins, and what it does not settle

The TESTED map (A) is the one the patch and the looks use, as decided. It agrees with the console profile that really lit the
heads on channels 1–10, including the two places where the equivalent chart is swapped (2/3 and 7/8). Past channel 10 it is
the *less* tested of the two and C contradicts it on 11–13. Nothing in the repo steps channels 11–16 one by one on a real unit
(`fixtures.json` says so: "ch11-16 are NOT tested until one unit is stepped 0..255 per channel on the desk").

**What this means for the show.** `src/rigbuild/fixtureTypes.js` gives ch 13 no default, so a look sends 0 there. If C is right,
a head given 0 on ch 13 emits no light, on all 18 beams. That is the one disagreement that can darken the night; the others
cost a wrong prism, focus or rainbow, or a dimmer that acts as a shutter.

## Walked on the real unit, on setup day

One UP-B380F on the floor, beam at a wall or the roof and away from people, the others unplugged, a console with a fader per
channel. About 30 minutes. Write each result on the patch sheet; the corrected map then replaces A in
`TESTED_CHANNEL_LISTS['UP-B380F']` with the date and the unit's label.

1. **ch 13 first.** Dimmer (ch 8) full, shutter (ch 7) at 255, everything else 0. Does it emit light with ch 13 at 0? Step ch 13 through 0–255 in steps of 16, then 145 exactly: what changes and where the light starts. Set the value that gives light as ch 13's default before any look runs.
2. **ch 7 and ch 8.** ch 7 at 255 and ch 8 stepped 0→255 (A: dimmer ramps, steady light). Then ch 8 at 255 and ch 7 stepped (A: strobe from 4, open at 255). On B the same two moves give a strobe and a dimmer instead.
3. **ch 11, 12, 14, 15** (prism, prism rotation, focus, rainbow/prism 2): step each 0→255 alone, note the channel where the prism goes in, which channel rotates it, which is focus, whether anything is a second prism or a rainbow.
4. **ch 2 and ch 3:** move ch 2 slowly: pan, tilt or a hair of pan fine? Then ch 3.
5. **ch 6 and ch 10:** is ch 6 frost or a second gobo; frost from 0 up (a ramp, or nothing until 128); the gobo at 30 and at 70 (A: gobo 6 and 14, B: 7 and 17), the first value of shake.
6. **ch 16 last, and only to confirm that 0 is the resting value.** Do not step it above 99 on a lit unit: on the equivalent chart 100–105 puts the lamp out, 240–255 resets the head, and a hot discharge lamp may not strike again until it has cooled (the equivalent manual asks 15 minutes before service). Read the ch 16 behaviour from the rental house's own chart instead.

Also asked of the rental house: the unit's own channel chart for the UP-B380F (it is the real answer to all of the above), and
the number of channels a UP-YZ31P smoke machine takes (1 or 2).

## Limits of this check

- B is a different maker's head. A match with it proves little; a mismatch proves nothing about the UP-B380F. It is a second opinion about what a 16-channel 380 W beam usually does.
- C is the console's profile, not a manual. It is evidence of what worked (heads lit and moved), not of what each channel means.
- Nothing here was seen on a unit. Everything in "Walked on the real unit" is owed.
- Table written by hand from the two sources above (A read from the file, B from `pdftotext` output of the PDF whose hash is given, C from the profile file); re-check it by opening them.
