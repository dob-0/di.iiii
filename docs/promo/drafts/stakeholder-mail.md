# Stakeholder mail template

Rules: short personal note, never newsletter tone. One recipient per mail, one concrete
reason *they* specifically would care, one link, one ask. Every send individually
approved before it goes out.

---

**Subject:** {a space, not a platform — e.g. "a communal XR rite you can enter from your phone"}

Dear {name},

{1–2 sentences of genuine personal context: where you met, their work you're
responding to, why them.}

I've been building di.iiii — an open-source studio for making shared XR spaces that
live entirely in the browser. Rather than describe it, here is one space built with it
that I think speaks to {their thing}:

{ONE link: br_id_ge / wcc / beyond-form / a space made for them}

There's nothing to install — it opens on a phone, laptop, or headset, and everyone
present shares the same live space.

{The ask — pick ONE:}
- I'd love 20 minutes to show you how {institution} could run an exhibition in it.
- Would this fit {festival/program}'s next open call?
- If you'd ever want to make something in it, I'll set a space up for you.

Warmly,
{name}
thedi.studio · https://di-studio.xyz

---

Variants to prepare per segment (fill after research lands):
- **Curator** — lead with br_id_ge, ask = 20-min walkthrough.
- **Festival / open call** — lead with no-install accessibility + past exhibitions, ask = submission fit.
- **Digital heritage org** — lead with the manifesto (30-year retrievability, open formats), ask = conversation.
- **WebXR advocate (browser vendor)** — lead with the tech (WebXR, open source), ask = community highlight.
