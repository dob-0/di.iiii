## 2026-10-10 — di follows keeps a space name apart from its address

- `di follows` printed the space `the-light-put-back` (18 characters) glued to its address: `the-light-put-backhttps://dev.diiii.xyz`. `platform-recordar` (17) got a single space. Seen on the installed di (0.4.4-dev.5b37c82f), read-only, 2026-10-10. A sweep had listed it as A38; it was still true when re-checked.
- Cause: `followList` in `scripts/di/ui.mjs` padded each name with `id.padEnd(18)` on its three kinds of row (following, not running, carried by another server) and hung the settings and file lines under a follow with `' '.repeat(18)`. A name of 18 or more characters leaves no padding. A space id can have 48 characters (`safeSlug` in `serverXR/src/spaceStore.js`).
- Fix: one width for the whole list, `Math.max(18, longest name + 2)`, used by every kind of row and by the indent. A list of short names prints exactly as before; a longer name widens the column for all rows. The widest case, a 48-character name, puts the addresses at column 52.
- Guard: `scripts/di/followFiles.test.js`, "di follows — the name column" (22 cases): names of 4, 17, 18, 19 and 48 characters on all three kinds of row, one address column per list at 20, 21, 22, 34 and 52, and the lines under a follow at the address column. Run on the old `ui.mjs` first: 17 of the 22 cases failed (29 tests in the file, 12 passed). After the fix: 29 of 29 passed.
- Not done: `ui.stageStatus` (same file) pads its row labels with a fixed `padEnd(11)`, which reads like the same fixed width for a screen name of 11 or more characters. Not run, not changed here; it is its own item.
- Not done: a very long name (up to 48) widens the column for every row, so a list with one such name wraps on an 80-column terminal. That is the price of measuring the column; capping it would bring the glued row back for the names above the cap.
