## 2026-10-28 — Solid walls: walk mode stops at the scene's own walls, trunks and models (part 2 of the Escape-the-Haunted-School parts)

- Before: walk mode only clamped the walker to 2D floor rectangles (`src/components/walkableAreas.js`: "Walk mode has never had wall collision"). A visitor strolled through every wall and trunk.
- Now: `worldState.solidWalls = { enabled: true }` makes the walker a capsule (radius 0.3 m, eye at `EYE_HEIGHT`, lowest point 0.3 m above the feet, highest 0.1 m above the eye) swept against the meshes the scene is drawn with. It slides along a wall, does not pass through one and does not get wedged in a corner. Off (`null`) is the default and then `walkStep()` is `confineToAreas()` exactly (unit-tested), so every existing project walks as before.
- Method: three-mesh-bvh `shapecast` + `closestPointToSegment` (the pattern of its own `characterMovement` example), steps of at most half the radius so a thin trunk cannot be jumped over, and contacts applied deepest first (a flat wall is two triangles; applying the diagonal edge's sideways push first made a walker pushing straight at a wall drift 0.4 m along it in 2 s, caught by the first test run).
- Files: `src/components/solidWalls.js` (maths, collider, keeper), `LiveProjectScene.jsx` (Walker takes `solidWalls`, calls `walkStep`), `src/shared/projectSchema.js` + `shared/projectSchema.cjs` (the field), tests beside each, `scripts/solid-walls/` (the moonlit-forest fixture and the look/measure script for `di-test-browser`).
- Floors stay as today in this part: no stairs, ramps or falling. Contacts whose normal is mostly vertical never push sideways, and anything under 0.3 m (a kerb, a floor slab) never blocks.
- Not in this part: flying and the headset's own locomotion (XrLocomotion) ignore the walls; skinned and instanced meshes are left out; a mesh that keeps moving is left out. Phones are not measured (owed), so they get a quarter of the triangle limit.
- Where it says what it is doing: `data-solid-walls` on the canvas (`on: 125 solid meshes, ...` or `off: ... over the limit ...`), also `console.warn` when off; stats on `canvas.__solidWallsStats`.
