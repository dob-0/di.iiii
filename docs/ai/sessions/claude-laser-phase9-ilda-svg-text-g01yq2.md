## 2026-10-20 — Laser File and Laser Text: ILDA 0/1/4/5, SVG paths and Hershey text as laser paths (laser Phase 9)

- Phase 9 of the raw laser workflow design (§5, ledger N645.1): two new nodes, registered as one block after Laser
  Merge in `nodeRegistry.js`, `nodes/index.js` and `allNodesExample.js`. Both are pure functions that return frame v2
  within FRAME_MAX_POINTS (2000 vertices; over it, every path keeps its ends and an even share, and Status counts the cut).
- **Laser File** (`laser.file`): `src` is an assetId (the Model/Video/Sound convention). The new `LaserFileFeed.jsx`
  fetches the asset and puts its bytes on the live side channel (`<node>:bytes`); `laserFile.js` tells ILDA from SVG
  by the bytes. `ildaFile.js` reads and writes ILDA formats 0, 1, 4, 5 and the format-2 palette; `svgPath.js` parses
  SVG 2 path data (M L H V C S Q T A Z, absolute and relative, run-together numbers and flags) and flattens curves
  (control points within the tolerance of the chord) and arcs (SVG 2 Appendix B.2.4–B.2.5; sagitta ≤ tolerance).
  Refusals are named (not-ilda, truncated-header, truncated-records, unknown-format, no-frames, not-laser-file,
  no-paths, the SVG grammar's own). The inspector offers every asset for its File (no media kind names .ild and .svg).
- **Laser Text** (`laser.text`): Hershey Roman Simplex, ASCII 32–126 (95 glyphs), generated by
  `scripts/laser/hershey-to-module.mjs` from npm `hershey` 2.1.7's `rowmans.jhf` (sha256 recorded), with the
  Usenet notice's acknowledgements (Hershey at NBS; James Hurt, Cognition) in the file, the Wiki and the provenance
  page `docs/ai/research/laser-phase9-provenance-2026-10-20.md`. Too-big text is made smaller, never clipped.
