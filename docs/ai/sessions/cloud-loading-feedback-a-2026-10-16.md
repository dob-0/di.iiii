## 2026-10-16 — loading feedback, design A: one load line, proposed

- The owner opened a big project in the Studio, saw only lamps for ~10 s (no hall, no toolbars, no word) and thought di had crashed. This branch is design A of two independent proposals; no code changed.
- `docs/ai/proposals/loading-feedback-A.md`: a file:line survey of every wait (Studio, room page, Spaces, Nodes, Perform, Light), then the design. Every wait reports to one register (`src/shared/loading/loads.js`, which grows out of `modelLoads.js`). One `LoadLine` draws it: a 2 px line under the appbar plus one mono line, "loading the hall · 2.1 of 3.3 MB". It never covers what is drawn, a failure stays in plain words, and it is not drawn on a projector output. The ring stays only where nothing is drawn yet, and gets its words drawn.
- `docs/ai/proposals/loading-feedback-A.html`: a static sketch of the Studio mid-load, plus every state and the phone. Opened in Chromium at 1280 and 390 wide and looked at.

Every route ring's words are for screen readers only.
