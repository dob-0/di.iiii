## 2026-11-10 — Output re-opens a camera whose track ended

- What: `src/map/MapSourceView.jsx` (`MapCameraSource`, used by `camera` and `stream` surfaces) now re-opens its camera by itself when the video track fires `ended` (or the stream goes `inactive`). It stops the old tracks, asks the device lookup of `MapStreamSource` again (`onStale`), and calls getUserMedia again after 0.5 s, 1 s, 2 s, then every 3 s, until live or until unmount / a different device. The first open retries the same way on NotReadableError, TrackStartError, NotFoundError, OverconstrainedError, AbortError when a device was asked for. NotAllowedError is not retried and keeps "camera not permitted".
- Why: on 2026-11-10 the projection output showed a phone camera (v4l2loopback fed by scrcpy) through a `stream` surface; after about an hour the track went to "ended" and the output stayed black until someone reloaded, though the camera was back (owner row N3522).
- Tested: `src/map/MapSourceView.reacquire.test.jsx`, fake timers and a faked `navigator.mediaDevices`: ended track leads to a second getUserMedia and the video gets the new stream; old tracks stopped; unmount in the back-off makes no further call; NotAllowedError is one call; NotReadableError then success. `npx vitest run src/map` and `npx eslint src/map` (no new warnings).
- Not tested: no real camera, scrcpy or v4l2loopback was used; no browser, so how a real browser orders `ended`, `inactive` and `devicechange` on a real unplug is unchecked. No desktop/phone surface screenshot. While a first-open retry runs for a device by id the surface stays a black video (no text), by design, so the mounted `<video>` and its effects survive.
