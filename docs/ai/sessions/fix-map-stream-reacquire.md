## 2026-11-10 — Output re-opens a camera whose track ended

- What: `src/map/MapSourceView.jsx` (`MapCameraSource`, used by `camera` and `stream` surfaces) now re-opens its camera by itself when the video track fires `ended` (or the stream goes `inactive`). It stops the old tracks, asks the device lookup of `MapStreamSource` again (`onStale`), and calls getUserMedia again after 0.5 s, 1 s, 2 s, then every 3 s, until live or until unmount / a different device. The first open retries the same way on NotReadableError, TrackStartError, NotFoundError, OverconstrainedError, AbortError when a device was asked for. NotAllowedError is not retried and keeps "camera not permitted".
- Why: on 2026-11-10 the projection output showed a phone camera (v4l2loopback fed by scrcpy) through a `stream` surface; after about an hour the track went to "ended" and the output stayed black until someone reloaded, though the camera was back (owner row N3522).
- Tested: `src/map/MapSourceView.reacquire.test.jsx`, fake timers and a faked `navigator.mediaDevices`: ended track leads to a second getUserMedia and the video gets the new stream; old tracks stopped; unmount in the back-off makes no further call; NotAllowedError is one call; NotReadableError then success. `npx vitest run src/map` and `npx eslint src/map` (no new warnings).
- Not tested: no real camera, scrcpy or v4l2loopback was used; no browser, so how a real browser orders `ended`, `inactive` and `devicechange` on a real unplug is unchecked. No desktop/phone surface screenshot. While a first-open retry runs for a device by id the surface stays a black video (no text), by design, so the mounted `<video>` and its effects survive.

## 2026-11-10 — Review round 1 (three findings fixed)

- Found: (1) MEDIUM: a lost track on a `stream` surface made `MapStreamSource` look the device up once; a miss (scrcpy restarting, no `devicechange` from v4l2loopback) swapped in the placeholder, unmounted `MapCameraSource` and its back-off, and nothing woke it up again. (2) MEDIUM: `tries` reset when getUserMedia resolved, so a feed that opens and ends within a second retried at 0.5 s forever. (3) LOW-MEDIUM: the default camera (empty ref) never retried after a live track ended, because the retry required a device id.
- Changed in `src/map/MapSourceView.jsx`: (1) `MapStreamSource` re-runs its lookup on its own timer (0.5, 1, 2 s, then every 3 s) while the label is missing, stops on match / unmount / ref change, and once an id was found a miss keeps the mounted camera instead of the placeholder; the hidden-label getUserMedia probe runs at most once per mount, retries only call enumerateDevices. (2) The back-off resets only after the track stayed live `STABLE_MS` = 5 s. (3) After a track was live, a re-open failure retries for the default camera too; a first-open failure keeps "camera unavailable"; NotAllowedError never retries.
- Tests (`src/map/MapSourceView.reacquire.test.jsx`, 17 tests): unmount during a back-off that was really pending (timer count before and after); refused permission on first open and on a re-open; `inactive` as a trigger; back-off 0.5/1/2/3/3 s with fake timers (camera and stream lookup); flapping feed escalates; reset after 5 s live; default camera after ended + one NotReadableError, and its first-open message; stream kind: label missing for two polls then back under a new id, re-opened with no `devicechange`; camera view not unmounted by a miss; probe at most once. Against `origin/dev` file: 14 of 17 fail; against the previous commit 1f06069be: 7 of 17 fail.
- Not tested: still no real browser, scrcpy or v4l2loopback; real timing of `ended`/`inactive`/`devicechange` unchecked; a device that stays missing for a long time keeps polling enumerateDevices every 3 s until unmount (by design); the case where a flapping feed lives between 1 s and 5 s is only covered by the 1 s case.
