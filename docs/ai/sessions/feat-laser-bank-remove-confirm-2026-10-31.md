## 2026-10-31 — laser desk: a link can propose removing looks, a person confirms (?remove=)

- Why: the laser routes answer only the desk's own page (a local-token guard, left exactly as it is), so scripts and agents could not delete looks. Ledger N1586 (owner: "yes delete all", 16 looks). His rule for such a limit: the agent asks, a person approves with one tap.
- What: the desk page accepts `/laser?remove=<id>,<id>,...` and asks at the top of Looks: "Remove these N looks from the bank?" with each look's name. An id not in the bank says "not found"; the look on the laser is kept ("kept: it is on the laser"). Nothing is sent until Remove is tapped. Remove uses the Edit pane's own delete (`desk/api/looks/delete`, one look at a time), then the page's own Save; the answer says "Removed N" with Undo (the desk's own undo, once per removed look, then Save) and the link is dropped from the address. Keep them closes it and sends nothing. A malformed list (ids outside A-Z a-z 0-9 - _, longer than 40, empty, or more than 200) is refused in plain words.
- Where: `serverXR/src/laser/ui/desk.js` (its own block: parseRemoveLink, planRemoval, paintRemoveAsk, showRemoveAsk, confirmRemoval, undoRemoval, keepRemoveAsk, startRemoveAsk; one line in refreshDesk; `saveAll` now returns the server's answer), `index.html` (#removeAsk), `desk.css` (.removeask), wiki entry "The laser desk", test `serverXR/src/laser/ui/deskRemove.test.js`.
- Not done: Undo reaches at most the desk's 100 undo steps, so a removal of more than 100 looks cannot be fully undone from the answer. The look was seen on the agent screen against a scratch serverXR, never on the stage Mac's own bank.

## 2026-10-31 — laser desk: a link can propose a look set, a person applies it with one tap (?set=)

- Why: the owner picks one of three redesigned sets of the show's laser looks by looking (Projector Room, doors 22:00); the stage machine's bank must take the chosen set with one tap, and the laser routes answer only the desk's own page (guards left exactly as they are).
- What: `/laser?set=<name>` (a-z, 0-9 and -, at most 60) reads `laser/ui/sets/<name>.json` ({ set, about, looks }) and asks at the top of Looks: "Apply the look set <name>?", the set's about lines, one row per look (name, id, now → new shape and colour, the other fields that change, the new look's picture drawn by the desk's own tile drawing, DiLaser). The look on the laser says "on the laser: changes at once"; a look the bank lacks says "new: will be created". Nothing is sent before Apply. Apply: `desk/api/looks/edit` per look (every field but id and follows), `desk/api/looks/paste` for a look the bank lacks (after the set's look before it; the desk names it and gives its id), then ONE `desk/api/store` of the last look written. Undo: `desk/api/undo` once per edit and paste, then the same Store. The bank is re-read before Apply and before Undo; if it moved, the page asks again. A malformed name, a missing file ("No set named …"), a bad or doubled id are refused in plain words.
- TRAP (known fix, the lead records it): the desk's Save never overwrites a saved look whose picture changed. `laserDesk.js` saveBank keeps the OLD look and adds the change as a NEW look ("… 2", a new id, `follows: null`) and moves the live look to it. So "edit each look, then Save" doubles the bank and leaves every scene link on the old looks: the show keeps playing the old looks. `desk/api/store` with `{ id, look }` writes the look into its own tile (same id, name and scene link) and writes the bank as it is (commit + desk.json). Store writes every unsaved change in place, so Apply waits while the bank has other unsaved changes. Guards: `deskSets.test.js` "Apply never posts desk/api/save" and "the scene links stay".
- Decision: flagged to the lead before building (the brief said edit then Save); the lead approved edit + paste + one Store, Undo = undo ×N + Store.
- Sets shipped (data, not code): `underground-a/b/c.json`, the three candidate sets with the first three non-empty lines of each NOTE.md as `about`; underground-c has an 11th look `muth-10-dots` (owner 19:5x: "a scene with only dots"). Every look normalises to itself (normaliseLook changes 0 fields); a test checks every shipped set. Served by the page's existing static mount (no new route, no catalogue entry).
- Where: `serverXR/src/laser/ui/desk.js` (block parseSetLink … startSetAsk; one line in refreshDesk), `index.html` (#setAsk), `desk.css` (.setlist), `ui/sets/`, wiki entry "The laser desk", test `serverXR/src/laser/ui/deskSets.test.js` (21 tests).
- Not done: Store is not an undo step on the desk, so the top bar's Undo after a set walks the edits back in memory while desk.json keeps the set until the next Store or Save; the panel's own Undo Stores again. A look the bank lacks takes the id the desk makes from its name; if that differs, the done line says "<id> was made as <id>". Seen on the agent screen against a scratch serverXR only, never on the stage Mac's bank.
- Seen (agent screen lane 2, scratch serverXR at its own address, 1278x758, bank seeded with the ten looks of set C): `?set=underground-a` asked with 10 rows and pictures, 0 POSTs in 1.2 s before the tap; Apply = 10 looks/edit + 1 store, 0 save, the bank stayed 28 looks, the live look stayed on, desk.json held set A; Undo = 10 undo + 1 store, desk.json back to C; `?set=underground-c` made `muth-10-dots` by paste (bank 29) and Undo took it away (28). The first look found Apply hidden under the Stage pane (the question was taller than the Looks pane; unit tests were green): `.setask` is now `flex: none; max-height: calc(100% - 52px)` with the about lines and the list scrolling.
