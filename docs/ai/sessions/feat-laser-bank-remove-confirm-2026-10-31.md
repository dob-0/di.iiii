## 2026-10-31 — laser desk: a link can propose removing looks, a person confirms (?remove=)

- Why: the laser routes answer only the desk's own page (a local-token guard, left exactly as it is), so scripts and agents could not delete looks. Ledger N1586 (owner: "yes delete all", 16 looks). His rule for such a limit: the agent asks, a person approves with one tap.
- What: the desk page accepts `/laser?remove=<id>,<id>,...` and asks at the top of Looks: "Remove these N looks from the bank?" with each look's name. An id not in the bank says "not found"; the look on the laser is kept ("kept: it is on the laser"). Nothing is sent until Remove is tapped. Remove uses the Edit pane's own delete (`desk/api/looks/delete`, one look at a time), then the page's own Save; the answer says "Removed N" with Undo (the desk's own undo, once per removed look, then Save) and the link is dropped from the address. Keep them closes it and sends nothing. A malformed list (ids outside A-Z a-z 0-9 - _, longer than 40, empty, or more than 200) is refused in plain words.
- Where: `serverXR/src/laser/ui/desk.js` (its own block: parseRemoveLink, planRemoval, paintRemoveAsk, showRemoveAsk, confirmRemoval, undoRemoval, keepRemoveAsk, startRemoveAsk; one line in refreshDesk; `saveAll` now returns the server's answer), `index.html` (#removeAsk), `desk.css` (.removeask), wiki entry "The laser desk", test `serverXR/src/laser/ui/deskRemove.test.js`.
- Not done: Undo reaches at most the desk's 100 undo steps, so a removal of more than 100 looks cannot be fully undone from the answer. The look was seen on the agent screen against a scratch serverXR, never on the stage Mac's own bank.
