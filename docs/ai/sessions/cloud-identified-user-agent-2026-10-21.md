CLI: auto-update health read, `di sync`/`di link`, `di follow`'s checks (fetch and address pin), `di login`. Credentials unchanged.
- Tests: 19 new cases (10 serverXR, 8 CLI, 1 login); 9 of them fail with the source reverted (shown once). Affected suites: 33 files, 402 tests pass (`scripts/di` follow/sync/login/channels/update, `serverXR/src/httpClient*`, `appVisitors`, `serverXR/src/follow`). Lint clean on the changed files.

The header also goes to non-di hosts reached through `httpClient` (OAuth, approval bot) — harmless, but it is a change.
