## 2026-09-30 — audit: how light and reflections reach a surface in the MOXIR ground rooms, and the knobs

- The owner looked at the minimal-ground room on his screen: too dark, and "there are also reflections". This branch is a read-only audit, one document: `docs/architecture/ROOM_LIGHT_AND_REFLECTIONS.md`. No code, data or render was touched; no browser was run; nothing was seen on a screen.
- Read from the code and the live project document: 53 lamps, 8 real lights (3 backstage and 2 column UP-B380F, 2 UP-250BSW, 1 column PAR), 45 beam-only; ambient 0.5, directional 0.22, ACESFilmic exposure 1, no environment map, shadows off, one baked wash of 10 PARs (117,580 bytes) baked for gs-white-cathedral.
- Findings, all from reading: (1) between the beams the room is computed near black (floor and deck about 3/255, columns about 17-22/255), and the 8 real lamps light about 27 m2 at most; (2) the wash stays the baked look's, and `washLevelOf` counts any group at a washing position, so the columns and press glow in five scenes whose PARs are out (gs-one-shaft 1.0, roof-reveal 0.9, slow-fan 0.8, cross-beams 0.4, laser-roof 0.35); (3) the roof, truss and floor have no wash surface type, so they are lit only by the 8 real lamps; (4) the real lamps are fixed per version at build time, so gs-blinder-hit has no real light and gs-one-shaft has one; (5) the room has no environment map, bloom or mirror: the "reflections" are most likely the additive cones, clipped hot spots, the emissive roof glazing and the wash decals (which one is unverified).
- The document ranks 12 knobs with file:line, current value, effect, risk and how each is measured, and sizes the two fixes: one wash mesh per look with switching by cue is M (a two-line first step, `washLevelOf` reading only the wash lamps, is S); the 8 brightest real lamps per look at runtime is L (a fixed pool of 8 lights, not mount and unmount, with fades).
- Owed: every brightness number is computed and needs one measurement on the owner's real screen (his iGPU Chromium, 14-19 fps); `look-probe.mjs` needs a GPU browser and would measure the machine's own GPU, not his; fps against real-lamp count is unmeasured; `full-ground`, cues 7-9 and the walk-mode light code were not read.
