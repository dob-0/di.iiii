## 2026-11-20 — the Monitor's Credits box no longer fails (and holds the snapshot) at the hourly slow check

- Why: the Credits block ran `di-credits status --json` under an 8 s limit. The tool keeps its probe verdicts for 3600 s and makes the real calls on the first run after that (12.1 s measured on aylmo; 2.2–2.9 s warm). So once an hour the box said "Unavailable here" and the whole snapshot waited the 8 s (snapshots of 20:42, 20:43, 21:14 UTC on 2026-11-19 were 8.0 s each). It also explains why a fresh `/admin#monitor` showed "Spaces and users" for a while: the Monitor tab appears only after the first full snapshot.
- What: `serverXR/src/monitorStore.js` keeps the last good list (`creditsMemo`) and renews it in the background; constants `CREDITS_FRESH_MS` 60 s, `CREDITS_STALE_MS` 5 min, `CREDITS_DROP_MS` 30 min, `CREDITS_WAIT_MS` 3 s, `CREDITS_COMMAND_MS` 40 s; new panel fields `checkedAt`, `stale`, `refreshError`, `checking`; store option `creditsWaitMs`. `scripts/di/monitorReport.mjs` passes the whole command limit (a one-shot process has no earlier list). `MonitorSection.jsx`: "Checking now" note, "read N ago; the latest check failed: …" note, tile "—" while checking and amber when old.
- Tested: `npx vitest run` on `monitorStore.test.js` (28 passed, 6 new; the 6 new fail on the old store), `MonitorSection.test.jsx` (52 passed, 3 new), `monitorReport.test.js` (16 passed, 1 new); eslint on the six touched files clean; `docs:ai:check` and the real tool with its cache switched off: see the PR.
- Not done: `di-credits` runs its probes one after the other (a di-atlas-live change); the hub drops `checkedAt`, `stale` and `checking` of another machine's credits (it keeps `ok, configured, rows, ready, total, error`); other machines' credits in the signed-in Monitor are still owed (ledger N5841.3).
