## 2026-10-21 — Flash Guard: a Raw node that holds light and picture values to WCAG 2.2 SC 2.3.1 (three flashes a second)

- New node `signal.flash`, label **Flash Guard** (numbers family, hidden, compute-only), next to Delay in the registry, the family map, `NODE_RUNTIMES`, the all-nodes example (col 0, row 18). Inputs Value, Full (1, or 255 for bytes), Limit (3); outputs Guarded, Rate (the incoming value's flashes in the last second), Limited (true while it held the value, and for one second after).
- The rule lives in ONE file, `src/project/nodes/flashRule.js` (no imports): WCAG 2.2 SC 2.3.1, a flash = a pair of opposing changes >= 10 % of full, 0.1 hysteresis, worst window shorter than 1 s, halved. `scripts/measure/flashrate.cjs` and `scripts/place/scenes_flash.cjs` now load it by dynamic `import()` instead of carrying their own copy (the laser_rules.py precedent). Their output is byte-identical before and after on 5 runs (flashrate on fx.js; scenes_flash on two committed scenes files with --json, on an over-limit file in text, and with --max 2 --json), compared with `cmp`.
- How it limits: it counts the changes it SENDS; a change that would put the last second over the limit is held 1e-6 short of the 10 % band until an older change leaves the window. A 10-a-second strobe comes out at <= 3 flashes a second (it holds 78 of 240 frames over 4 s). The limit cannot be switched off: Limit above 3, empty, 0, negative or not a number reads 3 (`flashLimitOf`); stricter (2, 1) is honoured. No enabled input exists.
- Found while testing: a swing of exactly 10 % (0.6 - 0.5 = 0.0999… in floating point) was not counted. The rule counts >= 0.1 - 1e-9 now; on the desk's /255 levels no step lands in that gap, so the scripts' numbers did not move.
- Tests: `src/project/nodes/flashRule.test.js` (11, from WCAG's definition: 2/s passes, 3/s at the limit, 4/s over, a 9 % swing ignored, exactly 10 % counted, the limit floor, plus a one-source scan of both scripts) and 8 cases in `nodeRuntimes.test.js`. Against a pass-through runtime 6 of the 8 node tests fail; against the old scripts the 2 one-source tests fail.
- Not done: no browser check (hidden node, no window); not wired into the desk engine (`serverXR/src/lighting/`) — the desk still has no limiter; WCAG's 0.80 "darker side" exemption and the red-flash rule are not modelled (the first makes the Guard stricter, the second is not checked at all); one Guard guards one number, not a whole picture or a rig.
