## 2026-10-21 — Cue: the project's cue list as wires, on one shared cue clock

- **One cue clock.** `shared/cueClock.cjs` (browser twin `src/shared/cueClock.js`, generated by
  `scripts/rigbuild/cue-clock-twin.mjs`, kept byte-identical by `src/shared/cueClock.test.js`, as
  `laserFrame.cjs` is): `cueClockAt({ list, epoch, loop, now, events })` gives index, progress, fading,
  next-in and ended from the cue list, a clock start and a person's Go/Back/Jump presses. Pure.
- **Three readers, one source.** The desk's runner (`serverXR/src/lighting/cuerun.js`) takes its
  next-cue rule and its new `progress` / `fading` (in `GET /api/cues` and the `cues` summary) from it;
  the show's clock (`src/rigbuild/showClock.js` `showStateAt`) now delegates to it — its 43 existing
  tests pass unchanged; `src/rigbuild/cueRun.js` re-exports its `nextCueIndex`.
- **The Cue node** (`signal.cue`, family numbers, next to Timer): Index (0-based, -1 before the first
  cue), Name, Look (the cue's `lightLook`), Progress, Fading, Fired out; Go, Back (`any`: a Button's
  press count, a Keyboard's boolean, Time's beat), Jump (a cue index, fires when it changes), Loop
  (unwired = `mappingState.loop`). With `showEpoch` and `showState.clockEpoch` set it reads the cue the
  show's clock puts every room on (test compares it with `showStateAt` at 7 instants).
- **Go and Back reach the room** through `fireCue` (`src/map/cueFiring.js`), the call the Perform
  window's Cues makes: `useCueNodeFiring` in `RawEditor` fires each cue a pressed node moves to, once
  (not from a preview card). A node nobody pressed fires nothing.
- Graph context now carries `mappingState` and `clockEpoch` (read only); `signal.cue` keeps the graph
  clock running (`CLOCK_DRIVEN_TYPE_IDS`).
- Progress runs over max(fade, hold), not fade + hold as the proposal said: the desk counts the hold
  from the fire, so fade + hold would disagree with the runner.

Not done, said plainly:
- Where a desk runs, the node does NOT read the desk's runner (`/api/cues`); it reads the document's
  show clock and its own presses. Following the desk needs a feed into `liveOutputs`.
- Presses are per window (frameMemory): a window opened later starts from the counts it finds. Two
  editors open on one project both fire the cue a press moves to (document ops are the same state;
  the desk's look is recalled twice).
- Changing Loop re-reads the whole press history under the new switch (the desk applies it forward).
- Not looked at in a browser, desktop or phone: this session had no dev server or screen.
