# Laser Phase 9 — provenance of every font, table and fixture (2026-10-20)

Phase 9 of [raw-laser-workflow-design-2026-10-14.md](raw-laser-workflow-design-2026-10-14.md) §5 (ledger N645.1):
`laser.file` **Laser File** and `laser.text` **Laser Text**. This page says where each piece of outside data came
from, under what terms, and on what date, and what could NOT be fetched.

## What could not be fetched (this session's network policy refused it)

| Host | Wanted | Result |
|---|---|---|
| `www.ilda.com`, `ilda.com` | ILDA Image Data Transfer Format, revision 011 (2014), `resources/StandardsDocs/ILDA_IDTF14_rev011.pdf` | **403 from the egress proxy** (CONNECT refused), 2026-10-20. Also refused: `web.archive.org`. |
| `paulbourke.net`, `emergent.unpythonic.net`, `ftp.funet.fi`, `www.ifp.illinois.edu` | the comp.sources.unix `hershey` distribution | 403, 2026-10-20 |

Consequence: **ILDA indexed colour is UNVERIFIED.** The record layouts and the default palette are second-hand
(below), and the ILDA round-trip tests hold the reader to this repo's own writer and to bytes built by hand
from the stated layout — not to a file or table from the ILDA. Re-check `src/project/nodes/laser.file/ildaFile.js`
against the rev011 PDF before quoting it as the standard.
