The rules, short

  • Show after 400 ms, keep for at least 600 ms. A fast cached load never flashes.
  • Say what (the object's own name, else a model, a picture, a video, a sound, the scene's light, the lamps, the tools) and how far (bytes, else files).
  • Never covers, dims or blocks what is drawn. Toolbars never wait for loads.
  • A failure stays, in plain words, until handled. No more console-only failures.
  • Not drawn on a projector output. Never white on a wall.
  • The ring stays only where nothing is drawn yet, and from now on it always has words.
