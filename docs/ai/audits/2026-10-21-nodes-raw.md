## Method

1. **Heuristics**: Nielsen's 10 usability heuristics (NN/g, "10 Usability Heuristics for User Interface Design"),
   severity 0–4 on Nielsen's scale (0 not a problem · 1 cosmetic · 2 minor · 3 major · 4 catastrophe).
2. **WCAG 2.2 AA**: axe-core 4.12.1 (already in the tree through `eslint-plugin-jsx-a11y`; injected with
   `page.addScriptTag`, so no new dependency) with the tags `wcag2a wcag2aa wcag21a wcag21aa wcag22aa`, on six
   editor states and the Out page. Plus the manual checks axe cannot do: keyboard reach (2.1.1), focus visible
   (2.4.7), a single-pointer or key way for every drag (2.5.7), target size (2.5.8; 44 px on touch per the brief).
3. **Device matrix**: mouse + keyboard 1440×900 at DPR 1 and 2 · touchpad: two-finger scroll as `WheelEvent`
   with `deltaMode 0` and `deltaX ≠ 0`, pinch as wheel with `ctrlKey: true` · phone `devices['iPhone 13']`
   (390×844, DPR 3, touch) · tablet 820×1180 touch · keyboard only. Touch pinch is CDP
   `Input.dispatchTouchEvent` with two points.
4. **Lint**: `eslint-plugin-jsx-a11y` (^6.10.2, rules as configured in `eslint.config.js`) over `src/raw` and
   `src/project/graph`, unique file:line.

Rerun: start a scratch stack (serverXR with `DATA_ROOT=<tmp> REQUIRE_AUTH=false`, Vite in front), then
`node scripts/audit/nodes-raw.cjs [base]`. It seeds its own project, prints one line per row, writes screenshots
to `.verify-surfaces/nodes-raw/`, exits 1 on any FAIL. `AXE_DETAIL=1` prints every axe node with its reason.

## What was walked, and what was not

Walked in Chromium, each row printed by the script: the canvas (wheel, touchpad swipe, ctrl+wheel pinch, CDP
touch pinch, tap), the add-a-node palette (`/`), the canvas menu (right-click), a selected card's settings column,

or ring), arrow-key card move, the Out page at 1440×900 and 390×844 (renders the scene; 0 axe nodes).

**UNVERIFIED** (not run here):
- any real device: a phone's own touch stack, a Mac or Windows touchpad's real wheel stream (inertia, `deltaMode`
  per OS), Safari's gesture events, a real screen reader (VoiceOver, NVDA) on the canvas's new group role;
- the 9 color-contrast checks axe could not decide (text over the grid and over gradients);
- the Director, Timeline, VJ deck, DMX Out, Laser Out and Agent windows: not opened by the walk (no seed for
  them); NR-11 and NR-12 come from reading the code;
- the touch wire drag and the long-press menu.
