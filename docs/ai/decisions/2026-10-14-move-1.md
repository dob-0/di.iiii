## What Move 1 is

The standard move, exactly as it was: the same speed in every direction, never read from what is under the pointer; the
automatic speed is the place's largest side / 40; Shift 5x faster, Alt 5x slower; the view turns around a point 10 steps ahead
(or where you stand); 100 px of a zoom drag is one step; flying covers one step a second. Code: `src/studio/navigation/freeMove.js`,
`useCameraNavigation.js`; the name and default: `src/studio/navigation/moves.js`. It is the default on every device that has not
chosen otherwise (`di.studio.navigation.move`).

The lead's numbers on the MOXIR v2-9 scene, measured 2026-10-14 with `scripts/nav/measure-pan-after-zoom.cjs`: 12 wheel notches
back = 69 m, about 5 m a notch, in = out; a 240 px pan after = 21 m.

## The rule

Move 1 is frozen. A later change of behaviour by any agent becomes a NEW move (Move 2, Move 3 ...) beside it, never an edit of
Move 1. `src/studio/navigation/move1.golden.test.js` holds Move 1's numbers as literals; when it fails, put the change back.
(`NAVIGATION_PRESETS` 'studio' / 'blender' are mouse-button maps, a different thing.)

## How to add Move 2

1. Add an entry to `MOVES` in `moves.js` (`id: 'move-2'`, `label`, `since`, `about`). The pickers in the Studio's Move block and the
   viewer's Move card appear by themselves at two entries (tested with a test-only second entry).
2. In `useCameraNavigation.js`, read the move (`useNavigationPreference().move`, pass it in like `speed` and `turn`) and give Move 2
   its own branch beside Move 1's. Do not touch Move 1's branch or its constants.
3. Give Move 2 its own golden test file. Update the Help note and the wiki entry.
