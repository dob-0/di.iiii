## Why

- It is the word a child of nine and a man of seventy-four already say; "a project with rules" is two nouns for one thing.
- It does not break the 2026-10-21 amendment. **game** is still a type of something, never a separate thing: an app on a machine,
  or a project in di.iiii. A game is always a project (or an app); it is never a fourth kind of thing beside space, project and scene.
- Steam and the Linux desktop (`Categories=Game`) use the same shape: a game is a kind of app, not a thing of its own.
