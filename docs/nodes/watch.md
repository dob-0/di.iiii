## Outliner (`view.outliner`)

**Inputs:**

## Monitor (`stream.monitor`)

TouchDesigner's viewer, as a window — wire any texture into Source and watch it live while you keep wiring. A picture operator (Camera In, Blur, …) cannot be watched here yet: its picture never leaves the GPU network (topRuntime.js), a known gap.

- `src` (texture) — Source
- `title` (string) — Title — default "Monitor"
