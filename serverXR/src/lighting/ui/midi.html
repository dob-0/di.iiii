2 — Auto-map

Wizard: press start, then move each fader/knob once and press each pad once, in the order you want them assigned. Each new control is bound as it arrives.
By number: no wiggling — takes every control seen so far and assigns them in numeric order.
 

Notes

Soft takeover is on. After arming, a fader does nothing until it passes through the desk's current value — so arming with your master fader at zero will not black the room out. Rows waiting to be caught show catch → value.

Open this page on the desk machine at localhost. Web MIDI is blocked on a plain LAN address, so http://192.168.…:8080/midi.html will show no devices.

Sends are coalesced to 25/s and issued one at a time, so a fast fader sweep cannot flood the desk or fight the 40 Hz DMX loop.

The map is saved in this browser and reloads with the page.
