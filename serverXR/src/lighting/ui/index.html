2 — Auto-map

Wizard: press start, then move each fader/knob once and press each pad once, in the order you want them assigned — each new control is bound as it arrives.
By number: no wiggling — takes every control seen so far and assigns them in numeric order.

 

Notes

Soft takeover is on. After arming, a fader does nothing until it passes through the desk's current value — so arming with your master fader at zero will not black the room out. Rows waiting to be caught read catch → value.

Web MIDI only works on localhost or https. Open the desk on the machine the controller is plugged into, at localhost — on a plain LAN address like http://192.168.… the browser refuses MIDI outright and the device list stays empty. Every other page of this desk works fine over the LAN; only this one needs the secure origin.

Sends are coalesced to 25/s and issued one at a time, so a fast fader sweep cannot flood the desk or fight the 40 Hz DMX loop.

The map is saved with the show, not in this browser — every surface on the desk gets the same controller layout.
