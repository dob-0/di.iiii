    Safety

    • Every cube starts disarmed. On is a person's yes. In phrase mode (DI_LASER_SIGN_OFF=phrase) the first On of a run asks for the laser safety officer's sign-off phrase.
    • A cube that is On with nothing to draw stays On, dark, until you press Off or Disarm all. With DI_LASER_DARK_OFF=auto (the older mode) it switches itself off after 5 s.
    • Disarm all, in the top bar, switches every output off in one click. The key switch on each cube always works.
    • The keep-in zone, the galvo guards, the still-beam guard and the signal-loss stop apply to the desk as to any other sender.
    • One sender per cube: a cube a Laser Out or the show holds refuses the desk until it lets go.

    Sign-off phrase

    The first On of a run takes the phrase the laser safety officer agreed.
